## Decision-Making Process

Both team members have the ability to initiate the decision-making process. The team will discuss available options and their potential outcomes, using a decision-making matrix or point system if necessary.

Shashwat will lead technical and architectural decision-making, while Rohan will lead project coordination and scheduling decisions. Significant decisions affecting project scope, requirements, or deliverables will be discussed and agreed upon by both team members.

If a resolution cannot be reached, the faculty advisor, Dr. Hrishikesh Vinayak Bhide, will be consulted for guidance.

## Conflict-Resolution Escalation Plan

Upon recognition of a conflict, team members will address it directly through respectful discussion using the crucial conversation framework: stating individual perspectives, confirming that both members are working with the same information, identifying shared motivations, and deciding on actions going forward.

If the conflict cannot be resolved through discussion between team members, the faculty advisor, Dr. Hrishikesh Vinayak Bhide, will be consulted at the next possible opportunity. If the advisor is unable to resolve the conflict, the course instructor will be consulted.
